# Mobile responsive findings

The local browser session confirmed the `/signin` surface was light at approximately 892×768. A deterministic Chromium DevTools smoke test then rendered `/`, `/signin`, `/signup`, `/member`, `/agent`, and `/admin` at a 375×812 mobile viewport after hydration delays. Every route reported `documentWidth === viewportWidth === 375` and `bodyWidth === 375`, so no horizontal overflow was detected. The protected entry routes correctly redirected to `/signin` without a session. The current headless Chromium screenshot encoder produced blank white PNGs even when the DOM was hydrated and the width assertions passed. Those unusable captures were removed from the commit; the route metrics are the authoritative responsive result, and the smoke-test source remains available for repeatable visual-tool follow-up.
